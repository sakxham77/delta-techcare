Student Repair Guide — Near Bennett University

Practical guidance for students facing a laptop or smartphone problem. All procedural content is illustrative for this classroom prototype.

This guide is educational content. It is not professional repair advice for a specific device. Always consult the manufacturer or a qualified technician before attempting repairs.

Laptop Repair Preparation Checklist

  • Back up important files before handing the device over
  • Write down the model number and serial number
  • Note the symptoms in a sentence or two
  • Sign out of email and payment apps where practical
  • Bring the charger and any relevant cables
  • Remove SD cards and USB drives

What to Do When a Laptop Screen Breaks

  1. Power down the device if it is still functioning
  2. Do not press or pry the shattered panel
  3. If it still boots to an external monitor, back up files immediately
  4. Note the model number before seeking repair
  5. Do not attempt to fix a screen without correct parts and experience

If Your Device Gets Wet

  1. Power off immediately — do not try to boot it to test
  2. Unplug the charger
  3. Do not use a hair dryer or oven
  4. Let it air dry in a cool, ventilated place
  5. Seek professional diagnosis as soon as possible

How to Back Up Files Before Repair

  • Copy documents to a USB drive or external disk
  • Upload photos and assignments to a trusted cloud account
  • Note any cloud accounts that require password resets after repair
  • Log out of personal accounts where possible

Basic Laptop Troubleshooting

  • Won't turn on: Check the charger, try a different outlet, hold power for 15 seconds
  • Slow performance: Check disk space, close background apps, restart
  • Overheating: Clean vents, use on a hard surface, avoid beds and sofas
  • Wi-Fi problems: Forget and rejoin the network, restart the router

Simulated Hostel Pickup Procedure
