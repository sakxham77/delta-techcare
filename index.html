Why This Prototype Was Designed This Way

Clarity first

Plain-language headings and clearly labelled demo notices — so visitors know what is real and what is not.

Local relevance

Content and structured data reference genuine nearby localities (Delta 1, Alpha, Pari Chowk) instead of keyword stuffing.

Accessible by default

Keyboard-navigable menus, ARIA labelling, focus states and readable contrast are baked into the design system.
